# LokalTools

LokalTools is a file workspace for iPhone and iPad with everyday PDF, image, video and file tools — merge and compress PDFs, make text searchable, resize and convert images, remove backgrounds, make passport photos, blur faces, compress and convert videos, rename in batches and find duplicates. Work with files on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in any folder you connect from the Files app.

Every tool runs **on your device**, and there is no account to create. Your files are not uploaded for processing or sent to an ad provider. Files you choose to save in iCloud Drive or a connected provider use that service. Advertising-enabled versions use Google AdMob with ad personalization disabled and may offer an optional ad-free subscription; see the Privacy Policy for advertising data and your choices. Versions with advertising disabled do not request ads or offer new ad-free subscriptions.
