# GrepTag

GrepTag searches inside your own TXT and EPUB books and tags them by what it finds.

Instead of organising a library by filename, you build keyword profiles for the things you care about, scan your files against them, and let the results become tags and ratings you can sort and filter on. It turns a folder of books you have never opened into a library you can actually navigate.

Everything — scanning, tagging and file management — happens on your device. No files, tags, keyword profiles, search terms or personal data are transmitted to our servers, and there are no analytics or telemetry.
