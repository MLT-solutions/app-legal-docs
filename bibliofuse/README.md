# BiblioFuse Reader

BiblioFuse is a cross-platform comic and ebook manager available through the App Store, Google Play and the Microsoft Store, with a companion app for Apple TV.

It reads CBZ, CBR, ZIP and EPUB files, and organises local and cloud libraries with metadata and tags. Beyond reading, it includes tools to compress, merge and convert comic archives to save space, and it can pull books from local folders or network (SMB) shares.

Your library stays yours. Files are synced through your own cloud storage — your private iCloud container on iOS and macOS, or your own Google Drive on Android — rather than through servers of ours. Reading progress and metadata sync across your devices the same way.

Pro offers vary by store. The App Store and Google Play offer a one-time Lifetime purchase and monthly or yearly subscriptions where available. The Microsoft Store offers a one-time Pro add-on for Windows.
