## Summary

Kitty Gambit is a cozy focus timer with an ambient board scene, companion customization, and optional background sounds. The app is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect personal information, analytics, timer history, companion choices, or usage data.

---

## 1. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, share, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files
- Focus session history, timer usage, or break patterns
- Companion, board, sound, or display preferences
- Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising identifiers
- Analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostic logs
- Payment information

Kitty Gambit does not require an account and does not connect to any MLOGICTECH server.

---

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device so it can remember your settings.

| Data | Storage Location | Purpose |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Work and break timer durations | UserDefaults | Restore your preferred focus timer settings |
| Selected board style and difficulty | UserDefaults | Restore the ambient board scene settings |
| Selected companions | UserDefaults | Restore your top and bottom companion choices |
| Sound and mute preferences | UserDefaults | Restore background sound and move-sound settings |
| Onboarding completion status | UserDefaults | Avoid showing onboarding every launch |
| Local session count | UserDefaults | Support in-app session behavior |

This data stays on your device. We do not receive or access it.

---

## 3. Notifications

Kitty Gambit may ask for permission to send local notifications so it can alert you when a focus or break timer phase finishes.

- Notifications are scheduled locally on your device
- Notification permission is controlled by iOS
- We do not receive notification tokens or send remote push notifications
- You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings

---

## 4. WebView and Game Scene

Kitty Gambit uses an embedded WebView to display the ambient board scene and companion animation.

- In App Store builds, the WebView loads bundled app files from the application package
- The bundled scene does not send your preferences or activity to us
- The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs

Developer/debug builds may load the local development server on `localhost`, but this is not used for App Store distribution.

---

## 5. Audio

Background sounds and board move sounds are generated or played locally on your device. Kitty Gambit does not record audio, access the microphone, or transmit audio data.

---

## 6. In-App Purchases

The current version of Kitty Gambit is offered as a free app and does not require an in-app purchase or subscription for its included features.

If paid features are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released. Any App Store purchases would be handled by Apple, and MLOGICTECH would not receive your payment card details.

---

## 7. Network Access

Kitty Gambit does not make automatic network requests to MLOGICTECH servers.

Network access may occur only when you choose to open external legal, support, or App Store links. Those pages are handled by your browser or Apple's services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

---

## 8. Data Deletion

All app settings are stored locally on your device. To remove them:

iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Kitty Gambit → Delete App

Deleting the app removes local preferences, onboarding state, and timer settings stored in the app container.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

Kitty Gambit does not collect information from any users, including children. The app is a general focus timer and is not directed at children under 13.
