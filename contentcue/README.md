# ContentCue Browser

ContentCue is a privacy-first reading browser, built for long reading sessions rather than general browsing.

It remembers where you were and can resume there on launch, marks links you have already followed on a site so you can tell new from read at a glance, keeps bookmarks for pages worth returning to, and lets you decide up front whether video autoplays and whether audio is muted.

Browsing happens entirely on your device through Apple's WebKit engine. ContentCue does not load any website by default — you choose the address or search you want to open. No browsing history, URLs, cookies or scroll positions are ever transmitted to us, because there is no server of ours to transmit them to. Your preferences, bookmarks and visited-link list are stored on the device.
