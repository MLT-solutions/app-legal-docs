## Summary

Hacked Account First Aid is an offline Windows checklist. The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or developer-operated server. The app itself does not transmit your checklist activity or device data.

The app can open websites in your default browser when you explicitly select a service link, the privacy policy, the terms, or the optional tip jar. Those websites are separate from the app and operate under their own privacy policies.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect or transmit:

- your name, email address, location, IP address, or device identifiers;
- the services, instructions, or checklist items you view;
- your checklist progress or preferences;
- browser data, passwords, cookies, account credentials, or command output;
- crash reports, diagnostics, analytics, or advertising data.

There is no account to create and no sign-in feature.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

The app stores only the following information in its private Windows application-data area:

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Completed checklist item identifiers | Restores your progress when the app is reopened |
| Language preference | Displays the app in your selected language |
| Appearance preference | Uses light, dark, or system appearance |

This information stays on the device. You can clear checklist progress from **Settings → Reset my progress**. Uninstalling the app removes its private application data according to Windows' normal app-removal behavior.

## 3. PowerShell Commands

The Advanced tab displays optional, read-only PowerShell commands. The app does not execute those commands and does not receive their output. If you copy and run a command, it runs in PowerShell under your Windows account and its output remains in that terminal session unless you choose to save or share it.

## 4. External Websites

Service links open the relevant provider's website in your default browser. When the browser opens a website, that provider may receive information normally sent by a browser, such as your IP address and browser data. The provider's privacy policy applies. MLOGICTECH does not receive information about which service links you open.

The app is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the services named in the checklist.

## 5. Optional Tips

The tip button opens PayPal in your default browser. The app does not process payments and does not receive card or bank details. PayPal processes the transaction under its own privacy policy. Depending on PayPal's service and your choices, PayPal may make transaction details such as a payer name, email address, amount, or message available to the recipient. No PayPal transaction information is stored inside the app.

Tips are optional and unlock no content or functionality.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## 7. Your Choices

Because MLOGICTECH does not collect app data, there is no server-side profile to access, export, correct, or delete. Local checklist progress can be reset in Settings, and all local app data can be removed by uninstalling the app.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will reflect changes to the app's actual data practices.
