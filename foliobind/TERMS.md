## 1. What This Application Does

FolioBind: EPUB Studio converts source files that you already possess into
EPUB ebooks. The supported source scope includes TXT, Markdown, DOCX,
fixed-layout PDF, CBZ, ZIP image archives, and CBR. Where a source document
refers to a picture — by web link or by cloud share link — the application may
retrieve that picture and store it inside the finished book.

**The application supplies no content.** It ships no catalogue, no library,
no search function, no list of websites and no links of any kind. It reads
only the documents you select, and acts only on references those documents
already contain, and only when you press a button.

The EPUB tools reject DRM-protected or otherwise unsupported encrypted
publications. They do not remove or circumvent DRM. Standard EPUB font
obfuscation may be preserved as part of an otherwise supported book.

## 8. Deletion and Data Loss — Important Disclaimer

**The application can permanently delete files.** Deleting a generated EPUB,
or a generated EPUB together with its exact matching source TXT, removes the
file(s) immediately and irreversibly — always after a confirmation dialog,
which this disclaimer applies notwithstanding.

- **You are solely responsible** for choosing which files to delete.
- **MLOGICTECH is not responsible** for any data loss, accidental deletion,
  or damage arising from use of these features.
- **Always keep independent backups** of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Deleting a matching source TXT alongside its EPUB is offered only when the
app can verify the exact source fingerprint. An EPUB overwrite (editing and
saving over the original) is built and validated locally, then the previous
version is copied to the app's Backups folder before a coordinated
replacement is attempted; a failure leaves the source in place. Backups,
generated books, and staged/imported files can each be cleared from Settings,
which also removes them irreversibly.
