# Hacked Account First Aid

Hacked Account First Aid is a step-by-step guide for the hours after an account has been compromised — what to secure, in what order, and where each provider keeps the setting.

It is a checklist and a set of directions, not a security scanner. It does not scan your device, detect anything, or remove anything. If you need malware removal, use a product built for that.

The app is **fully offline**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, and it works with the network turned off — which matters here, because people often open this app immediately after an incident, sometimes while deliberately disconnected. Your progress is stored on the device only.

Providers move their settings pages regularly. Where the app and a provider disagree, the provider is right — follow their own help pages. The About screen shows when the app's steps were last checked, and telling us which service looks wrong gets it corrected in the next update.
