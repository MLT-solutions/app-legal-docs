# Support — Hacked Account First Aid

The app works entirely offline and collects nothing, so there is no account to recover and
no data of yours for us to look up. Most questions are one of the following.

## A step does not match what I see

Providers move their settings pages regularly. Where the app and the provider disagree,
**the provider is right** — follow their own help pages. The app shows when its steps were
last checked, on the About screen.

If you tell us which service and what you actually see, the step gets corrected in the next
update.

## My progress disappeared

Ticks are stored on the device only. They do not sync between devices and are not backed
up to a server, by design — nothing about which accounts you were securing leaves your
phone. Reinstalling the app clears them.

## Is this antivirus?

No. It is a checklist and a set of directions. It does not scan your device, detect
anything, or remove anything. If you need malware removal, use a security product built
for that.

## Something is wrong, or a link is broken

Email **support@mlogictech.com** with the app version (Settings → About) and the screen you
were on. Reports about wrong menu paths or dead links are especially useful.
