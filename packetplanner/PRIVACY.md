# **Privacy Policy - PacketPlanner (Angpao Planner)**

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-02

## **1. Introduction**

PacketPlanner / Angpao Planner (\"the App\") is provided by Modern Logic Tech Solutions (\"we\" or \"us\"). We prioritize your privacy and transparency.

## **2. Data Collection & Storage**

**Local Storage Only:**

The App stores all your data **locally on your device**. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. We have no servers that receive your data.

* **Plans:** Your recipient names, packet amounts, groups, and records of packets you received are stored locally using device storage (Capacitor Preferences API and browser localStorage).
* **Settings:** Your language preference, theme choice, region, and currency are stored locally.
* **Calculator Data:** Note types and inventory counts are stored locally.

**Optional Backup (Pro):**

Pro users can turn on automatic backup. This is **off by default** and you control it in Settings.

* **iPhone and iPad:** When enabled, each of your plans is saved as a JSON file into your own private iCloud Drive container for this App, which appears in the Files app under "PacketPlanner". This data goes to **your** Apple iCloud account, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We have no access to it and cannot read it. Apple's iCloud sync makes these files available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
* **Android:** When enabled, each plan is saved as a JSON file in the App's private storage on your device. Sending a copy to Google Drive is a manual action you initiate from the share sheet; nothing is uploaded automatically.
* **Windows:** When enabled, each plan is saved as a JSON file in a **PacketPlanner Backups** folder inside your own Documents folder. The files stay on your PC. If that folder happens to be inside a folder you already sync (OneDrive, for example), your own sync setting decides where the copies go — the App itself uploads nothing.
* Turning backup off stops new backups. Files already written remain in your iCloud Drive, device storage or Documents folder until you delete them.

**No Accounts, No Analytics:**

The App has no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not run analytics or tracking on your planning data.

## **3. Payments & In-App Purchases**

**One-Time Purchase:**

The App offers a one-time \"Pro\" upgrade that unlocks:
- Multiple plans, so you can keep a separate sheet for each year (Free users limited to 1 plan)
- Unlimited groups (Free users limited to 1 group)
- Optional automatic backup (iCloud on iPhone/iPad, device storage on Android, Documents folder on Windows)
- Export to Excel
- Backup/Restore data
- Ad-free experience (Android only)

On **Windows** there is no in-app purchase: the Microsoft Store version is a paid app, offered with a free trial period set in the Store listing, and every feature above is unlocked from first launch. Payment and trial licensing are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store.

* **Payment Processing:** All financial transactions are processed directly by the **Apple App Store**, **Google Play Store** or **Microsoft Store**. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card information or billing details.
* **Transaction Data:** We may receive anonymous transaction receipts to validate your \"Pro\" status. This data does not include your personal identity.

## **4. Third-Party Services**

**Google Play Services & Apple StoreKit:**
* Used for purchase validation on their respective platforms.
* We do not collect analytics or tracking data through these services.

**AdMob (Android Free Users Only):**
* The free version of the Android app displays banner ads at the bottom of the screen provided by Google AdMob.
* AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad targeting. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.
* Pro users do not see any ads.

## **5. Permissions**

The App requires the following permissions to function:

* **Storage Access (Android):** Required to export/import backup files and Excel reports.
* **Network Access:** Required to validate premium licenses and serve ads (free Android version only).
* **iCloud (iOS, optional):** Required only if you turn on iCloud backup, to write your backup files into your own iCloud Drive container.
* **Documents folder (Windows, optional):** Used only if you turn on automatic backup, to write your backup files into Documents\PacketPlanner Backups.

## **6. Data Deletion**

You can delete all app data at any time by:
* **iOS:** Settings → General → iPhone Storage → PacketPlanner → Delete App
* **Android:** Settings → Apps → PacketPlanner → Storage → Clear Data
* **Windows:** Settings → Apps → Installed apps → PacketPlanner → Uninstall

On iPhone and iPad, if you enabled iCloud backup, delete the backup files separately in the Files app under iCloud Drive → PacketPlanner, or from Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Deleting the app does not automatically remove files already saved to your iCloud Drive.

On Windows there is no iCloud backup. If you enabled automatic backup, the files in Documents\PacketPlanner Backups are yours and are left in place; delete that folder to remove them.

## **7. Children's Privacy**

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. We do not collect data from users, so the app is safe for all ages.
