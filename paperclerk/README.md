# PaperClerk

PaperClerk files your documents for you. Point it at a receipt, an invoice or a statement and it reads the details that matter — vendor, date, amount, document type — and files the document accordingly, instead of leaving you to rename and sort everything by hand.

The reading is done entirely on your device by on-device OCR and AI extraction — on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and Windows. PaperClerk does not upload documents for OCR or AI extraction. That distinction matters for this kind of app: the documents people most want filed automatically are usually the ones they would least want uploaded to someone else's server.

Nothing is collected, stored on our servers, or transmitted to us — not the contents, names or paths of your documents, not the text pulled out of them, and no analytics or telemetry.
