# PaperClerk

PaperClerk files your documents for you. Point it at a receipt, an invoice or a statement and it reads the details that matter — vendor, date, amount, document type — and files the document accordingly, instead of leaving you to rename and sort everything by hand.

The reading is done by Apple Intelligence **on your device**. No document, filename, or piece of extracted text is sent anywhere. That distinction matters for this kind of app: the documents people most want filed automatically are usually the ones they would least want uploaded to someone else's server.

Nothing is collected, stored on our servers, or transmitted — not the contents, names or paths of your documents, not the text pulled out of them, and no analytics or telemetry.
